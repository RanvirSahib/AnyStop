FAQ

## Quick answers

Is the timetable live?

Not yet. Today it shows planned timings. Live rider updates are on the roadmap.

Which cities and routes are covered?

We’re starting with the Chandigarh, Ludhiana, Jalandhar and Ambala corridor and adding more stops over time.

Do I need an account?

No. You can search as a guest. An account will let you save favourite routes.

Can I book tickets here?

For buses that allow online booking, we link you to the booking site. Most government buses are paid on board.
